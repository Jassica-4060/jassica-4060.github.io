# 品牌應用素材

製作方式：內建 image_gen。此圖為虛構品牌應用示意。

## 最終提示詞

Use case: product-mockup.
Create one polished portfolio illustration asset, landscape 4:3, PNG. It depicts a fictional CoolBe corporate merchandise/stationery collection.
Input image 1 is the STRICT VISUAL STYLE reference and actual CoolBe logo reference. Input image 2 is ONLY the original mascot identity reference; do NOT include its page layout, annotations, speech bubble or 3D rendering style.
Style: extremely clean flat vector-like illustration, pure solid fills, thin blue-gray outlines, square/rectangular modular geometry, generous negative space, NO realistic photography, no 3D, NO gradients, NO fabric/paper textures, no drop shadows, no dramatic perspective. Match reference 1's quiet beige background exactly approximately #F3F0E8, white objects, brand sky blue #68B1E8, slate #6C757D, navy accents. Opaque beige background throughout, not transparent.
Composition: neatly balanced modular arrangement of FIVE unmistakable merchandise objects with comfortable margins, all fully visible, in one coherent scene. Large T-shirt on the LEFT taking roughly 40% of width; to the right arrange business cards at upper middle, a lanyard ID badge at upper right, a greeting card/envelope set below middle, and a round mascot coaster at lower right. No surrounding dashboard/card containers or numbered section headings. It must be minimal enough to read at a thumbnail size.
1. A WHITE short sleeve CoolBe T-shirt, flat front silhouette with subtle collar outline and a small accurate CoolBe logo on the chest. Do not draw human or hanger.
2. Two simple rectangular BUSINESS CARDS, one white with CoolBe logo, the other muted blue with minimal white contact-like short bars. A slight overlap is okay, no angled perspective. No real personal details.
3. An employee ID BADGE with blue lanyard, white rectangular badge, original CoolBe logo, simple square generic avatar and 2-3 small muted skeleton bars. No invented personal text.
4. A GREETING CARD with an envelope behind it. Minimal white and pale blue rectangles, real CoolBe logo, one simple geometric celebratory motif such as 4-point sparkle; optional exact text 'THANK YOU' only, no additional slogans.
5. A ROUND gift COASTER featuring a simplified FLAT depiction of CoolBe's actual mascot 酷客: white egg-shaped floating robot, dark slate visor, small white side fins, NO LEGS, small antenna with a yellow C on top. Keep recognizable to reference 2. Render it as flat solid geometry printed within a pale blue round coaster, with a fine blue outline. No cup or mug.
Preserve the original CoolBe symbol: two opposing C/B-like semicircular thin outlines in charcoal and blue, with the thin blue vertical stem and horizontal middle bar; use exact 'CoolBe' capitalization, charcoal 'Cool' and blue 'Be'. Logo is the only recurring brand text. Avoid random words, watermarks, title, logo redesigns, extra objects, gradients, shadows and clutter.
